How It Works

Three steps
to an easier party

No mixing, no last-minute bottle runs, no cleanup. Order, host, and leave the dispenser where it is.

01

Choose your flavours

Pick from twelve signature cocktails. Each dispenser holds one flavour. Order a few and choose a different flavour for each.

02

We deliver it ready to pour

Your LED dispenser arrives filled with a removable cooling tube fitted in the lid. We set it up before we leave. Nothing to mix or assemble.

03

You host, we collect

Pour, host and enjoy the night. We come back the next day and take the dispenser away. No cleaning, no packing down.

The Centrepiece

The SpiritDrop
dispenser

01 Cocktail Chamber

20 drinks

An approximately 4.75 L prepared cocktail batch, one flavour per dispenser. That pours 20 ready-to-pour drinks.

02 Ready-To-Pour Tap

Lift and fill

Arrives pre-mixed. Lift the tap, fill the glass. Nothing to measure, nothing to stir.

03 Removable Cooling Tube

Fitted through the lid

A removable cooling tube drops through the lid and sits separate from the cocktail, helping keep the dispenser chilled.

04 LED Base

Built to glow

A colour-cycling LED base lights the liquid from below.
