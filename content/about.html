Our Founders

Who we are

SpiritDrop was founded by best friends Dmitri Mendis and Jamain Hassarati in Sydney, with a simple goal: make cocktails at events easier without taking away from the experience.

We built SpiritDrop around ready-to-pour cocktail dispensers, straightforward delivery and setup, and a product designed to become part of the event itself.

What started as an idea between two friends has developed into a service focused on making event drinks simple, distinctive and easy to enjoy.

Our Story

Born from
better nights

After one too many parties spent mixing drinks instead of enjoying them, we built the thing we wanted at our own events: cocktails already mixed, chilled and ready to pour.

Today SpiritDrop delivers ready-to-pour cocktail dispensers from our Liverpool base to 21sts, engagements, house parties and functions. Consistent drinks, premium presentation and zero cleanup.
