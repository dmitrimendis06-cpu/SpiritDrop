About SpiritDrop

SpiritDrop is a Sydney-based cocktail delivery brand built around one simple idea: make serving drinks at events easier without making the experience feel ordinary.

We prepare ready-to-pour cocktails in our signature dispensers, deliver and set them up for the event, then return the following day to collect the equipment.

The result is a cocktail setup designed to be simple for the host, distinctive for the event and ready when guests are.

Independently owned. Sydney operated.

Why SpiritDrop
exists

Hosting an event already comes with enough to organise.

Drinks should not require someone spending the night measuring, mixing and constantly restocking a makeshift bar.

SpiritDrop was built as a simpler alternative: one prepared cocktail system that arrives ready for the event, looks like part of the setup and removes much of the work from serving a group.

How it works

What we do

  1. 01Prepared
    Cocktails are prepared in advance in the flavour you choose for each dispenser.
  2. 02Delivered
    SpiritDrop delivers the dispenser to your approved event address, in the delivery window you pick at checkout.
  3. 03Set up
    Basic dispenser setup is included. We position it and leave it ready to pour.
  4. 04Ready to pour
    Each dispenser holds 20 ready-to-pour drinks.
  5. 05Collected
    We return the next day to collect the equipment.
