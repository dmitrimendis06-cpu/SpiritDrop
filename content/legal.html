Terms & Conditions Draft

Draft revised 26 August 2026. Not yet in effect — no effective date applies until these policies are finalised and published.

Confirmed terms

  • Each SpiritDrop dispenser is $200. There are no quantity discounts.
  • A $50 refundable equipment bond applies per dispenser, in addition to the $200 price. It is returned in full once the dispenser is collected without damage.
  • Each dispenser contains 20 ready-to-pour drinks in a single selected flavour. Customers ordering more than one dispenser may choose a different flavour for each.
  • The dispenser is returnable equipment supplied on loan for the booking, not a purchased item.
  • Delivery is charged by location. The fee depends on which configured postcode zone the delivery address falls into and is shown before you confirm your booking. It is not included in the $200 dispenser price.
  • Basic setup is included. We deliver the dispenser, position it and leave it ready to pour. A bartender or staff member does not remain on site.
  • Collection is always the next day. You select a next-day collection window at checkout.
  • A full delivery address is required — street address, unit or apartment where applicable, suburb, state and postcode.
  • The person receiving the delivery must be 18 or over and may be required to provide valid ID.
  • The purchaser is responsible for ensuring alcohol supplied by SpiritDrop is not unlawfully supplied to minors and is consumed in accordance with NSW liquor laws.
  • Alcohol is supplied for off-premises consumption.

Privacy Policy Draft

Draft revised 26 August 2026. Not yet in effect — no effective date applies until these policies are finalised and published.

What we plan to collect

  • Your name, email address and phone number, so we can confirm and coordinate your booking.
  • Your date of birth, so we can check that alcohol is only sold to adults aged 18 and over.
  • Your delivery address and any delivery instructions.
  • The name of the adult receiving the delivery, if that isn't you.
  • Your order details — dispensers, flavours, add-ons, dates and windows.

How your information is handled

To run the service, SpiritDrop will use third-party providers such as a payment provider, hosting provider, email provider and address-verification service. Your information will be shared with those providers only to the extent needed to deliver your order, and we will only use it for the purposes described here or where the law requires it.

Marketing emails will be optional. Signing up for updates is a separate, deliberate action — placing an order will never subscribe you automatically, and every marketing message will include an unsubscribe link.

Pending review — do not publish without these
  • Named service providers, their locations and any overseas data transfers.

  • Cookie and analytics disclosure once analytics are added.

This prototype site stores only your cart contents (flavour and quantity) in your browser. Your name, email, phone, date of birth, address and recipient details are not saved to this device and are not transmitted anywhere.
