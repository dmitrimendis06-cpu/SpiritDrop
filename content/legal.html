Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Who we are

SpiritDrop is operated by the J HASSARATI & D.S MENDIS partnership, ABN 84 187 494 574. The NSW packaged liquor licensee is Dmitri Mendis. Licence number: LIQP770018577.

You can contact us at spiritdropjd@gmail.com.

Price, bond and delivery

  • Each SpiritDrop dispenser is $200. There are no automatic quantity discounts.
  • All prices are in Australian dollars and include any GST applicable by law.
  • A $50 refundable equipment bond applies to each dispenser. The bond is returned after collection where the dispenser is returned without damage.
  • Each dispenser contains 20 ready-to-pour drinks in a single selected flavour. If you order more than one dispenser, you may choose a different flavour for each.
  • Delivery is charged by location. The fee depends on the postcode zone of the delivery address and is shown before you confirm your booking. It is not included in the $200 dispenser price.
  • Optional add-ons, where available, are displayed with their applicable price and quantity during ordering and are added only when selected by you.

Delivery, setup and collection

  • Basic setup is included. We deliver the dispenser, position it and leave it ready to pour. SpiritDrop does not provide an onsite bartender or remain at the event.
  • We do not offer same-day delivery.
  • You must provide an accurate, full delivery address (street address, unit or apartment where applicable, suburb, state and postcode), appropriate access information, and ensure an eligible adult recipient is available during the agreed delivery window.
  • Collection is always the next day. You must make the dispenser available during the agreed next-day collection window.
  • If collection cannot occur because you have not provided reasonable access or made the equipment available, SpiritDrop will contact you to arrange another collection. Any additional collection cost must be reasonable and will be disclosed to you before the additional collection occurs.

Age, ID and responsible supply

No Alcohol can be sold or supplied to anyone under 18. It's against the law.

  • The purchaser must be 18 or over. We ask for your date of birth when you book.
  • The person receiving the delivery must be 18 or over and may be required to provide valid ID.
  • The purchaser is responsible for ensuring alcohol supplied by SpiritDrop is not unlawfully supplied to minors and is consumed in accordance with NSW liquor laws. Minors may be present at private events, but alcohol must not be unlawfully supplied to them.
  • Alcohol is supplied for off-premises consumption.

Refusal of delivery

SpiritDrop may refuse to supply or complete a delivery where doing so would be unlawful or inconsistent with its liquor licence or responsible-service obligations. Delivery will be refused where:

  • the recipient is under 18;
  • required age or identity verification cannot be satisfactorily completed;
  • the recipient appears intoxicated;
  • the intended recipient does not satisfy the applicable delivery requirements;
  • delivery would otherwise breach NSW liquor laws or a condition of SpiritDrop's liquor licence; or
  • liquor cannot lawfully be delivered to the location.

Alcohol will not be left unattended where lawful delivery requirements have not been satisfied. Payment for an order does not require SpiritDrop to complete a delivery that would be unlawful. What happens to your booking in that case is set out under Cancellation & Refunds.

The dispenser and the equipment bond

  • The dispenser is returnable equipment supplied for your booking. It remains the property of SpiritDrop throughout the booking.
  • You must take reasonable care of the dispenser while it is in your possession and follow any use and safety instructions we supply.
  • The $50 equipment bond may be applied toward damage caused during the booking. Normal fair wear and tear is not chargeable.
  • Where a dispenser is lost, not returned, or damaged beyond fair wear and tear and the reasonable repair or replacement cost exceeds the bond, SpiritDrop may seek recovery of the reasonable additional repair or replacement cost. Any additional amount will reflect reasonable actual loss or cost and will not operate as a penalty.

Your responsibilities after delivery

After lawful delivery and basic setup, the purchaser and recipient are responsible for:

  • reasonable care of the equipment;
  • lawful use of the alcohol;
  • preventing unlawful supply of alcohol;
  • making the dispenser available for next-day collection; and
  • following the use and safety instructions supplied.

This does not apply to loss caused by SpiritDrop itself.

Complaints and disputes

If you have a concern or dispute, please first contact SpiritDrop at spiritdropjd@gmail.com. We will attempt to resolve complaints reasonably and in good faith.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

What we collect

  • Your name, email address and phone number, so we can confirm and coordinate your booking.
  • Your date of birth, so we can check that alcohol is only sold to adults aged 18 and over.
  • Your delivery address and any delivery or access instructions.
  • The name of the adult receiving the delivery, if that isn't you.
  • Your order details: dispensers, flavours, add-ons, dates and delivery and collection windows.

How we use it

We use your information to provide and administer your booking, to meet our legal and liquor-licence obligations, to keep accounting and tax records, to resolve disputes, and to prevent fraud and protect the security of our service.

Date-of-birth and age-verification information is collected for lawful alcohol-sale and compliance purposes. SpiritDrop will not use age-verification information for unrelated marketing purposes. We do not use your date of birth or delivery information for unrelated targeted marketing.

Marketing

Purchasing does not automatically subscribe you to marketing. Signing up for updates is separate and optional, and every marketing message includes a way to unsubscribe.

Service providers

SpiritDrop may use service providers to help run the business, for example for website hosting, payments, transactional email, address validation, analytics, security and order administration. We share personal information with them only to the extent needed for those purposes.

Some technology and service providers may process or store information outside Australia. Where this occurs, SpiritDrop will take reasonable steps required by applicable privacy law in relation to those disclosures.

Cookies and browser storage

This website currently uses essential browser storage only, where necessary for site functions: it keeps your non-sensitive cart contents (flavour and quantity) in your browser, and, if you arrive through a campaign link, the campaign tags for that visit. The website does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies. If we introduce them, we will update this policy at the same time and ask for your consent where required.

How long we keep it

SpiritDrop retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for providing and administering orders, legal and regulatory compliance, accounting and taxation, resolving disputes, and fraud and security prevention.

Business and transaction records may be retained for the period required under Australian taxation and other applicable laws, which is generally at least five years for many business records. Age-verification information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected or where retention is required by law. Personal information that is no longer reasonably required will be deleted or de-identified where legally appropriate.

Access to your information

You may request access to the personal information SpiritDrop holds about you by contacting spiritdropjd@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before releasing information. We will respond within a reasonable period and in accordance with any applicable privacy-law requirements.

Correcting your information

You may ask SpiritDrop to correct personal information that is inaccurate, incomplete, out of date or misleading. Please send requests to spiritdropjd@gmail.com.

Privacy complaints

Privacy complaints should be sent to spiritdropjd@gmail.com. Please describe the issue and include relevant booking or order details where appropriate. SpiritDrop will investigate the complaint and respond within a reasonable period.

Where applicable, you may also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC). Contacting SpiritDrop first does not remove your right to complain to the OAIC or another external body.

Cancellation, Rescheduling & Refunds

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law apply in addition to this policy and override it where applicable. Nothing in this policy limits those rights.

Cancelling a booking

If you wish to cancel, please notify SpiritDrop at least 24 hours before the scheduled delivery time by emailing spiritdropjd@gmail.com.

  • At least 24 hours before delivery: SpiritDrop may process the applicable booking refund or, where agreed with you, reschedule the booking.
  • Change-of-mind cancellations within 24 hours of delivery: a refund is not automatically guaranteed, because product preparation, stock allocation and delivery arrangements may already have occurred. SpiritDrop may offer rescheduling or credit depending on the circumstances.

Refunds

Refunds are returned to the original payment method unless another lawful arrangement is agreed. Where SpiritDrop approves a monetary refund, SpiritDrop will initiate the refund to the original payment method promptly. The time taken for the funds to appear may depend on your bank or payment provider.

The $50 equipment bond per dispenser is refundable and is returned after collection where the dispenser is returned without damage.

Rescheduling

Rescheduling requests should be made at least 24 hours before scheduled delivery and are subject to availability. SpiritDrop may decline repeated or unreasonable rescheduling requests. If SpiritDrop agrees to a new date, the revised booking date becomes the applicable booking date for the order.

Weather and events outside our control

Where severe weather, unsafe access, road closures or another circumstance outside SpiritDrop's reasonable control prevents safe delivery, SpiritDrop will contact you as soon as reasonably practicable. Depending on the circumstances, SpiritDrop may:

  • arrange another suitable delivery time;
  • reschedule the booking; or
  • provide an appropriate refund for the service that cannot be supplied.

Nothing in this clause limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

If delivery can't be completed

If delivery cannot be completed because nobody eligible is available, access cannot reasonably be obtained, or the supplied address or instructions are incorrect, SpiritDrop will attempt to contact you. A redelivery may be offered subject to availability. Any additional redelivery charge will reflect a reasonable additional delivery cost and will be disclosed before you agree to the redelivery.

If age or ID can't be verified

If the recipient cannot satisfy the applicable age or identification requirements, SpiritDrop will refuse to supply the alcohol, and the alcohol will not be left unattended. SpiritDrop will contact the purchaser about the failed delivery. Any refund, credit or redelivery will take into account the circumstances and any reasonable costs already incurred, subject always to the Australian Consumer Law and NSW liquor-law requirements.
