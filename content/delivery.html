The Process

From order
to collection

Four stages. We handle the heavy lifting at both ends.

  1. 01

    Order & pick a date

    Choose a flavour for each dispenser and lock in your event date and delivery window at checkout. $200 per dispenser plus a $50 refundable equipment bond per dispenser.

    Earliest date is the next day
  2. 02

    We deliver & set up

    Our driver arrives in your chosen window with the dispenser chilled, filled and ready. We set it up on your bar or table.

    Windows shown at checkout
  3. 03

    You pour & enjoy

    Lift the tap, fill the glass. Twenty consistent, ready-to-pour drinks with nothing to measure.

    All night, your night
  4. 04

    Next-day collection

    We return the next day to collect the dispenser. You choose your next-day collection window at checkout. Leave it where it is and we do the rest.

    Next-day pickup included

Receiving Your Delivery

The 18+ & ID rules

  • The order must be received by a person aged 18 or over
  • Valid photo ID may be required on delivery
  • We can't leave alcohol with anyone underage or intoxicated
  • Alcohol is supplied for off-premises consumption only
  • Minors may be present at your event, but alcohol must not be supplied to them
  • The dispenser is returnable equipment, collected next day
